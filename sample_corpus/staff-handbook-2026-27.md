## Daily sign-in

All staff sign in at the main office by 7:35 AM on regular schedule days. On
two-hour-delay days, sign-in moves to 9:35 AM. Substitute staff also collect
their room keys and daily folder at sign-in.

## Leave requests

Planned personal leave is requested in the staff portal at least three school days
in advance. Sick leave is reported by 6:15 AM through the absence line or portal so
a substitute can be assigned. Bereavement leave follows the negotiated agreement,
Article 9. Leave during the first and last week of the school year and on
professional-development days requires building principal approval.

## Dress code

Staff dress is professional and appropriate to assigned duties. Fridays are
district spirit-wear days. Physical education, maintenance, and food-service
staff follow their department dress standards.

## Emergency drills

Each building conducts one fire drill per month while school is in session, plus
two tornado drills and three safety (lockdown) drills per school year. Teachers
post the evacuation route beside their classroom door and carry the red class
roster clip during every drill.

## Mandatory reporting

Every staff member is a mandated reporter under Ohio law. Suspected abuse or
neglect is reported immediately to children's services and to the building
principal. Do not investigate independently; document what was observed and when
the report was made.

## Visitors

All visitors, including former staff and family members, sign in at the main
office and wear a visitor badge while in the building. Staff who see an unbadged
adult should greet them and walk them to the office.

## Building hours

Buildings are open to staff 6:30 AM to 9:00 PM on school days. Weekend access
requires 24-hour notice to the head custodian and use of the staff entrance only.
