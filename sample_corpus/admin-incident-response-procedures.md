## Threat or safety report received

1. The building administrator assesses immediacy with the SRO or, if unavailable,
   the superintendent's office. Imminent threats: initiate the building response
   protocol first, document second.
2. Non-imminent reports are logged in the incident system the same day, with the
   reporting party, time, and exact wording of the concern.
3. The superintendent is briefed before dismissal on any incident involving law
   enforcement contact, media presence, or a parent-notification decision.

## Device examination approvals (Policy 5136 §4)

Only the principal may approve examination of a confiscated device, in writing,
before the examination occurs. The written approval records the reasonable
suspicion basis, the scope examined, who was present, and the outcome. File the
record with the incident log the same day; the December board report aggregates
these records.

## Media contact

All media inquiries route to the superintendent's office. Building staff do not
confirm or deny incident details. The standard holding statement is in the crisis
binder, tab 4.

## After-action

Within five school days of any building-level incident response, the
administrator files a one-page after-action summary: timeline, notifications
made, what worked, and one improvement for the next drill cycle.
