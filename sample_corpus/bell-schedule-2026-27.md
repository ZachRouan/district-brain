## Early-release Wednesday

Every Wednesday is an early-release day for staff professional learning.
Classes run on a compressed schedule with 40-minute periods; student dismissal
is 1:45 PM, and staff PD runs 2:00–3:30 PM. There is no Lunch B on Wednesdays;
all students eat during a single lunch block 11:15–11:45 AM.

## Two-hour delay

On a two-hour delay, Period 1 begins at 9:50 AM, periods run 30 minutes, lunch
blocks are unchanged, and dismissal remains 2:58 PM. Morning preschool and
morning career-center transportation are canceled on delay days.

## Elementary (Maple Ridge Elementary)

Doors open 8:35 AM; instruction 8:50 AM – 3:20 PM; dismissal by bus lane at
3:20 PM and parent pickup at 3:30 PM. On early-release Wednesdays, elementary
dismissal is 2:05 PM.
