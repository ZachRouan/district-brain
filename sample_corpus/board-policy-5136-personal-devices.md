# Board Policy 5136 — Personal Electronic Devices

*Maple Ridge Unified School District · Adopted 2019-06-11 · Last amended 2026-03-10*
*(Synthetic sample document for demonstration. Not a real district policy.)*

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs student possession and use of personal electronic devices,
including phones, tablets, smartwatches, and wireless earbuds, on district
property and at district-sponsored events.

## 2. Permitted use

Students may possess personal electronic devices at school. Devices must remain
silenced and stored out of sight during instructional time unless the supervising
teacher explicitly permits their use for an instructional activity. Use is
permitted before school, after school, and during a student's assigned lunch
period in the cafeteria or commons.

## 3. Confiscation

A staff member may confiscate a device when its use disrupts instruction, violates
the Acceptable Use Policy (Policy 7540), or compromises the privacy or safety of
another person. Confiscation follows these steps:

1. **First offense:** the device is held by the teacher and returned to the student
   at the end of the class period, with a verbal reminder of this policy.
2. **Second offense:** the device is delivered to the main office and returned to
   the student at the end of the school day. The incident is recorded in the
   office log.
3. **Third and subsequent offenses:** the device is held in the main office for
   release only to the student's parent or guardian, and an administrator schedules
   a conference.

## 4. Searches of confiscated devices

A confiscated device may not be searched by staff. Building administration may
examine a device's contents only with the documented approval of the principal and
only when there is reasonable suspicion that the device contains evidence of a
violation of law or board policy, consistent with Policy 5771 (Search and Seizure).
Every such examination is documented in writing, including scope and findings.

## 5. Refusal to surrender a device

Refusal to surrender a device when directed is treated as insubordination under
the Student Code of Conduct, Section 12. The staff member should not physically
take a device from a student; instead, refer the matter to building administration.

## 6. Liability

The district assumes no responsibility for loss, theft, or damage to personal
devices brought onto district property.

## 7. Amendment history

The March 2026 amendment (Board resolution 2026-41, adopted 2026-03-10) added
Section 4's written-documentation requirement and clarified end-of-day return in
Section 3, step 2.
