# Inclement Weather & Calamity Day Procedures

*Maple Ridge Unified School District · Updated 2025-11-03*
*(Synthetic sample document for demonstration.)*

## Decision timeline

The superintendent decides on delays or closures by 5:30 AM after consulting the
transportation supervisor's road report and neighboring districts. Notification
goes out through the mass-notification system, the district website, and local
media by 5:45 AM.

## Two-hour delay

Staff report two hours after their regular time; the building opens to students
20 minutes before the delayed start. Morning preschool and morning career-center
transportation are canceled. Breakfast service is not offered on delay days.

## Closure (calamity day)

All student activities, practices, and facility rentals are canceled unless the
athletic director and superintendent jointly announce an exception by noon.
Twelve-month staff report unless the closure announcement states otherwise;
a two-hour reporting delay applies to twelve-month staff automatically.

## Blended-learning make-up days

After the third calamity day in a school year, subsequent calamity days convert
to remote-learning days. Teachers post assignments by 9:00 AM in the learning
management system; student work is due within one week. Paper packets prepared
each October cover students without home internet access.

## Early dismissal for weather

When weather deteriorates during the school day, the superintendent may order an
early dismissal, normally announced by 12:30 PM. Elementary students are not
released to empty homes; the emergency-contact release list in the office copy
of the student information system governs.
