# Board Policy 9150 — Visitors to the Schools

*Maple Ridge Unified School District · Adopted 2015-01-13 · Last amended 2024-11-11*
*(Synthetic sample document for demonstration. Not a real district policy.)*

The Board welcomes visits by parents, community members, and other guests, and
also has the duty to keep instruction free of disruption and buildings secure.

1. **Sign-in.** Every visitor reports to the main office immediately on entering
   a building, presents photo identification on request, states the purpose of
   the visit, and wears the issued visitor badge visibly at all times.
2. **Classroom visits.** Classroom observation requests are arranged with the
   principal at least one school day in advance so instruction is not disrupted.
   The principal may limit length, timing, or frequency of visits.
3. **Escort.** Visitors to instructional areas are escorted or checked on by
   office staff. Staff members who encounter an adult without a badge should
   greet the person and accompany them to the main office.
4. **Removal.** The principal or designee may withdraw permission to visit and
   direct any person to leave when the person's conduct disrupts school
   operations. Refusal to leave is referred to law enforcement as trespass.
5. **Board meetings.** This policy does not limit public attendance at meetings
   of the Board of Education, which is governed by Policy 0169.1.
